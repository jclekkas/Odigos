# Independence

Odigos is a tool for car buyers. It is not a dealership, a lender, or a lead seller.

- It does not sell cars and it does not take referral fees from dealers or lenders.
- A quote you submit is not sent to a dealership.
- The project does not sell leads. Derived, aggregated pricing signals may be kept to study fees, as the privacy policy describes. Raw quotes are not sold.
- State rules ship in this repository. A cap is applied only when that file says the state has one. The file marks rows that still need a primary source.
- The hosted site at odigosauto.com is free to use. Self-hosting uses your own model key. The software does not contain the operator's keys.

Nothing here is legal advice. Confirm a fee cap or a tax rate with the state, or with a lawyer, before you treat a verdict as a statement of the law.
