The site falls away to the south, which decided almost everything else. Rather than cut a pad into the slope, the house steps with it: a low entry wing holding the private rooms, then a single tall volume where the ground drops, glazed the full width so the canyon reads as the far wall of the living space.

The deep overhang is not a gesture. It is what makes a west-facing glass wall habitable in August.

Materials are few and repeated — board-formed concrete at the base, white plaster above, bronze at every opening, rift-sawn oak inside. The roof is a single shallow plane carried past the glass on both sides. What the drawings promised is what was built; the changes during construction ran to a single page.
