Four steps,
in that order.

The sequence in the drawing above is not decoration. It is how the company works, and the reason we can say what a house will cost and when it will be finished.

01

Acquire

We buy sites directly, usually before they are listed. Orientation, grade and privacy decide the purchase — a difficult lot with the right light beats an easy one without it.

02

Design

Drawings are finished before we break ground. Materials, openings and details are resolved on paper, where changing them costs a morning rather than a month.

03

Build

We hold the build contract ourselves and keep the same trades from one house to the next. The people who priced the work are the people who do it.

04

Deliver

We finish the punch list before handover, not after. Every house leaves with its drawings, its warranties and a direct line back to us.

We work with a small number of private investors, project by project. Every conversation starts the same way: a site we are looking at, the drawings we would put on it, and an honest account of what we think it takes to build. Nothing is offered here — this page exists so that the right conversation can start.

Participation is by invitation and follows a conversation, not a form. If it turns into something, you should expect to meet the people running the build, walk the site, and see the drawings before anything is signed.
