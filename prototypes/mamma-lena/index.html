Our story

From Naples to Maryland.

In 1994 the Varriale family left Italy for Washington, when Carlo took a post at the Italian Embassy. Lena had already spent years in Italian kitchens — among them Assunta Madre in Rome.

In 2013 she and her sons opened a small trattoria on Clopper Road. No hospitality group. No concept deck. Just Lena’s recipes, her family in the dining room, and a kitchen she has never left.
